    Demos

    Four interactive pages, each rewritten from one of my Python projects (a Flask app, a scraper, a few notebooks) to run in your browser. Nothing to install and no account needed. Pick one to open it.

    ISS Tracker

    Where the International Space Station is right now, on a world map, with its ground track drawn in as it moves. Updates every 5 seconds.

    Live data Map

    Open ISS Tracker

    Strasbourg Movie Showtimes

    Today's showtimes at the 5 Strasbourg cinemas, filtered by genre, language or format from a terminal-style command line.

    Updated daily Terminal

    Open Movie Showtimes

    France Temperature Map

    Current temperature across all 96 metropolitan départements, as a colour-coded map. Hover or tap a département for its feels-like temperature and sky conditions.

    Live data Map

    Open France Temperature Map

    Powder Diffraction Rings

    Pick a crystal powder and an X-ray tube. The Debye–Scherrer rings, a detector image and a θ–2θ scan are computed from Bragg's law as you change them.

    Physics simulation Interactive

    Open Powder Diffraction Rings
